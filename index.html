About the Event

This one-day event will explore how the potential of genetic associations can be harnessed through the integration of deep molecular phenotyping (proteomics, single-cell and spatial expression and AI-enriched perturbation predictions). The programme features talks from each of the major skin disease molecular data projects, a keynote lecture, selected lightning talks by early career researchers, and opportunities for discussion and networking.

If you are a pre-doctoral or post-doctoral researcher working in this area, please consider submitting an abstract. Presenters of selected abstracts will receive a travel award and receive free registration to ESDR.
